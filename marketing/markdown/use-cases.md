# Where your entire organization executes and outperforms 
Configured to every team inside your firm, from deal to back office 

By Team 

## One platform configured to how each team inside your firm already works 
01 Deal Teams 

Underwrite faster and with deeper conviction. The system reads the data room, builds the comps, and surfaces the pattern from prior deals. 

VDR analysis 

Data room indexed as it fills, with gaps and material changes flagged. 

Presentation review 

Decks cross-referenced against the data room in minutes. 

Expert interviews 

Operator and customer calls transcribed and synthesized into the deal record. 

IC memo drafting 

Sourced memos in your firm's format, ready for partner review. 

Company profiles & strips 

Standardized profiles built from filings, news, and primary research. 

Financial benchmarking 

Targets benchmarked against your prior deals and sector peers. 

Public & private comps 

Comparable sets assembled from live market data and transaction history. 

Portfolio monitoring 

Deviations from thesis surfaced before they appear in a board meeting. 

02 Investor Relations 

LP communications drafted in your firm's voice and sourced from underlying fund data, with DDQs answered in hours not weeks 

DDQ automation 

Every question answered from prior responses and the firm's record, in hours instead of weeks. 

Quarterly letters 

Drafted to your template from current fund and portfolio data. 

Capital calls & distributions 

Notices generated and tied to the underlying schedule. 

Fundraising materials 

Pitch decks and data rooms updated against live performance. 

03 CFO & Fund Finance 

Valuations, audit support, and capital tracking running against live source data. Data flowing into your portfolio management tools without manual entry. 

Portfolio management automation 

Portfolio data entered into iLEVEL, eFront, and other systems automatically. 

Quarterly valuations 

Models rebuilt from current financials and comparables. 

Audit support 

Every figure traced to source. Auditors review answers, not sourcing. 

Capital tracking 

Commitments, calls, and distributions reconciled automatically. 

Management reporting 

Fund performance, fees, and waterfall calculations on demand. 

04 Legal & Compliance 

Contract review, regulatory filings, marketing material review, and KYC running with the speed and consistency of an extra senior associate that never sleeps. 

NDA & contract review 

Redlines against your firm's playbook in seconds. 

SEC Marketing Rule review 

Pitch decks, fund tearsheets, and LP materials checked against Rule 206(4)-1 before they go out. 

Regulatory filings 

Form ADV, PF, and ILPA reports drafted from fund data. 

KYC / AML 

Counterparty diligence aggregated, flagged, and tracked. 

Side letter tracking 

Every commitment indexed, searchable, and surfaced when relevant. 

05 Portfolio Value Creation 

Deploy VectorShift inside your portfolio companies to accelerate top-line growth and strip cost out of the back office. The same platform that powers your firm becomes a lever inside every asset. 

Revenue acceleration 

Sales intelligence, customer research, and outbound enablement deployed across the portfolio. 

Back-office automation 

Finance, HR, and operations workflows automated to take cost out of SG&A. 

Customer & market intelligence 

Continuous research on customers, competitors, and market shifts across every company. 

Standardized operating reporting 

KPI reporting from each portco flowing into the fund's monitoring stack. 

06 Office Operations 

The day-to-day operational work that keeps a firm running, handled in the background. Less time on logistics, more time on judgment. 

Scheduling & coordination 

Meetings booked, availability checked, and follow-ups sent on your behalf. 

Travel & expense 

Itineraries booked, expenses categorized, and reports filed automatically. 

Internal knowledge 

Firm processes, policies, and prior decisions surfaced when anyone needs them. 

Inbox & calendar triage 

Priority items surfaced, routine threads handled, and time protected for the work that matters.
