## Security and compliance, *by design *
For the IT, security, and compliance buyer. VectorShift deploys on terms institutional finance requires 

i. 

### Single-tenant deployment 
Run in your VPC, dedicated cloud, or fully on-prem. Your data never leaves your perimeter. 

ii. 

### Permissioned by design
