# Support

> Get help from the VectorShift support team - hours, SLA, and contact options

The VectorShift support team helps with setup, integration, troubleshooting, and platform questions. Choose the option that best fits your needs below.

<Info>
  **Support hours:** 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM ET (USA)<br />
  **First response SLA:** 24 hours
</Info>

## Contact options

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Live chat (Intercom)" icon="comments" href="https://app.vectorshift.ai/home">
    Best for quick questions and urgent issues. Open the chat widget in the bottom-right corner of the VectorShift platform to create a support ticket or speak with the team in real time when available.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Email support" icon="envelope" href="mailto:support@vectorshift.ai">
    **[support@vectorshift.ai](mailto:support@vectorshift.ai)** - Use for non-urgent questions, detailed requests, or when you need to attach screenshots or logs. We respond within 24 hours.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
