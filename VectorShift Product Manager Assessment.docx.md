**VectorShift Product Assessment**

**Product Manager (Platform)**

## **Overview**

This assessment is about how you evaluate a real product. You will spend time in the VectorShift builder, form your own view of what should be improved, and make the case for it. There is no trick and no hidden right answer. We are interested in what you notice, how you prioritize, and how clearly you argue.

## **Access**

You would have received another email inviting you to VectorShift. Once you accept the invite, you can access the platform at [https://app.vectorshift.ai/projects](https://app.vectorshift.ai/projects). If you have any trouble getting in, reply to this email.

## **The task**

1. **Explore the builder view.** This is where users create and manage everything on the platform. What you explore and how deep you go is up to you. The main areas are:

* **Projects:** where workflows and agents live. Workflows are built on a drag and drop canvas by adding nodes (LLMs, integrations, logic), connecting them, running them, and deploying the result. Agents handle tasks more autonomously using the tools you give them.  
* **Knowledge bases:** where you upload large amounts of data (files, URLs, live-synced sources like Google Drive or Notion) and make it searchable, so workflows and agents can pull from it to answer with your data.  
* **Tables:** structured, spreadsheet-style data that workflows and agents can read from and write to.  
* **Skills:** reusable instructions and capabilities you define once and attach to agents.  
* **Marketplace:** pre-built templates you can clone and adapt instead of starting from scratch.  
* **Analytics:** where you track how your workflows are running and being used.

2. **Form a view on who VectorShift is for.** Based on what you see, tell us who you think the product is for and what the top use cases are that it can solve. A short, opinionated answer is better than a safe, generic one.

3. **Build one of those use cases.** Pick one use case from your list and build it as a working workflow or agent, end to end. The point is that you have actually used the product, not just looked at it.

4. **Identify the top things you would fix or improve about the builder view.** Rank them. For each one, tell us: what the issue is (be specific: which screen, which interaction, which behavior), why it matters and who it affects, and what you would do about it.

5. **Make the case for your \#1.** Go one level deeper on your top item: why it is first on the list, and how you would approach fixing it with an engineer, including how you would know the fix worked. Include a prototype of how you think it should be solved, in whatever form is fastest for you: a rough mockup, a sketch, a Figma frame, or something hacked together with AI tools. Rough is fine; we care about the idea, not the polish.

## **Deliverable**

A short document (2 to 3 pages) containing:

* Your view on who VectorShift is for and its top use cases

* A link to, or screenshots of, the use case you built

* Your ranked list of issues (aim for 5, quality over quantity)

* The deep dive on your \#1, with your prototype of the solution

* **Bonus (not required):** a brief competitor analysis. Pick 1 or 2 platforms you consider competitors, what they do better, and which gaps you would prioritize closing.
